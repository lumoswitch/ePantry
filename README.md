**ePantry - Pantry Item Expiration & Stock Management App**

ePantry is a smart, easy-to-use mobile application designed to help users manage their pantry by tracking the expiration dates of food items and notifying them when items are about to run out. The app helps reduce food waste, saves money, and ensures that you never run out of essential items in your kitchen again.

**Features**

1. Track Expiration Dates
        Add Items Easily: Quickly add pantry items by scanning barcodes or entering product details manually.
        Expiration Notifications: Receive reminders when items are close to their expiration dates, helping you use them before they spoil.
2. Track Low Stock Items
        Track Quantity: Enter the quantity of each item in your pantry and track it over time.
        Low Stock Alerts: Get notifications when items are running low, so you can replenish them before they run out.
3. Pantry Overview
        Sort & Filter: View your pantry items by expiration date, category, or item name. Easily see which items need attention.
        Quick Search: Find any pantry item quickly using the app’s search functionality.
4. Barcode Scanning
        Fast Entry: Use your device’s camera to scan barcodes of items to automatically input the name, expiration date, and other relevant details into the app.
5. Pantry Item Categories
        Categorize Items: Sort your pantry items by categories such as canned goods, grains, spices, snacks, etc., for better organization.
6. Inventory History
        Track Past Items: Keep a history of items you’ve used or thrown out, so you can plan your future purchases accordingly.
7. Shopping List Integration
        Quick Shopping List: Automatically generate a shopping list based on the items that are about to expire or running low.
8. Multiple Pantry Support
        Multiple Locations: Track pantry items across multiple locations like your kitchen, office, or even a second home.

**How It Works**

Add Items: Start by adding the food items in your pantry. You can add items manually or scan the barcode to fill in product details.

Set Expiration Dates: For each item, set an expiration date. If no date is available, the app will use a standard shelf life based on the type of product.

Track Stock Levels: For each item, enter the current quantity you have. Update the stock level as you use the items.

Get Alerts: The app will notify you when items are nearing their expiration or when the stock is low.

Shop & Replenish: Use the app’s shopping list feature to purchase items that are running out or near expiration.

**Benefits**

Reduce Food Waste: Stay on top of expiration dates and use up pantry items before they spoil.

Save Money: By tracking pantry stock, you avoid buying duplicates or unnecessary items.

Improve Meal Planning: Know exactly what ingredients you have on hand, so you can plan meals efficiently.

Stay Organized: Keep your pantry items neatly organized with categories and expiration dates.

**Installation**

Download ePantry from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).
Open the app and create an account or log in if you already have one.
Begin adding your pantry items and customize your settings.
